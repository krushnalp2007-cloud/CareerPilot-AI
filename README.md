## 🎯 The Problem

Students often know the career they want, but don't know:

**What skills do I need?**  
**Which skills do I already have?**  
**What am I missing?**  
**What should I learn next?**  
**How should I prepare for interviews?**

CareerPilot AI brings these answers together in one platform.

---

## 💡 The Solution

CareerPilot AI analyzes:

**Career Goal + Student Skills + Resume Evidence**

and turns them into:

**Skill Insights → Skill Gaps → Learning Direction → Interview Preparation**

---

## ✨ What You Can Do

### 📊 Skill Analysis
Understand the skills required for your target career and identify your current gaps.

### 📄 Resume Review
Upload your **PDF or DOCX resume** and receive AI-powered feedback based on its content.

### 📚 Learning Path
Get a structured learning direction based on the skills you need to strengthen.

### 🎤 Interview Preparation
Generate interview questions tailored to your selected career and skill gaps.

### 📈 Career Dashboard
View your career readiness and key skill insights in one place.
